# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary users are people who want to turn their own trips and adventures into articles or standalone web pages. Readers experience the finished stories.

## Product Purpose

Give authors a web app to assemble a trip story from their own writing and photos, add route data when available, and preview the finished page.

## Positioning

A trip-story editor grounded in the author's own material. A route map can help readers follow the journey when geography is part of the story.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The current implementation renders one NorthCape 4000 journal with photos and an interactive GPX atlas. The next UI focus is the authoring experience for a new story. Draft storage, publishing, and export are open decisions.

## Evidence on Hand

The NorthCape 4000 journal is a reference for what a finished story can look like. Its manuscript is in `data/article.pdf`, its photographs are in `data/photos/`, and its route recordings are in `data/northcape-4000/gpx/`.

## Product Principles

- Let authors start with their own trip and keep control of the story they tell.
- Make maps and route data optional so the editor works for trips without GPS recordings.
- Let authors preview the story as readers will see it. Each finished page should make sense on its own.
