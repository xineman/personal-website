# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Readers who want to follow the author's NorthCape 4000 cycling trip and understand what the journey was like.

## Product Purpose

Publish a creative, standalone article about the trip. It is the first page of a future personal website and should tell a complete story before the rest of the site exists.

## Positioning

A firsthand account of a single ride, grounded in the author's written story, route data, and photographs.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The initial scope is one article page. The broader scope of the personal website and the publication target are open.

## Evidence on Hand

- `data/article.pdf` contains the author's manuscript.
- `data/photos/` contains original photographs from the trip.
- `data/northcape-4000/gpx/` contains 25 GPX files for the trip.

## Product Principles

- Use the author's article as the source for the story.
- Use the trip's own route data and photographs to help readers follow the journey.
- Make the article complete as a standalone page and suitable for inclusion in the personal website later.
